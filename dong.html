01 · THE PLACE

WHERE INDIA
MEETS FIRST LIGHT.

Dong is a small Meyor village about 7 km from Walong on the left bank of the Lohit River. Its eastern position makes it famous for witnessing the first sunrise in India.

The experience is bigger than the sunrise itself: a pre-dawn mountain walk, hanging bridges, pine forests, river valleys and a quiet glimpse of life in one of Arunachal's most remote corners.

03 · THE ROAD

THE EASTERN
EDGE TAKES TIME.

TINSUKIA→TEZU→HAWAI→WALONG→DONG

The official district route lists Tinsukia via Tezu, Hayuliang and Walong. Walong is the practical base for exploring Dong, Kibithoo and nearby attractions. Expect long mountain-road days and build in weather and road-condition buffers.

04 · PLAN SMART

GO FOR THE
RIGHT MORNING.

OCTOBER – APRILCLEARER WINDOW

A commonly recommended window for the sunrise experience, with generally better road and trekking conditions. Always check current local conditions.

MONSOONBUILD BUFFER

Heavy rain can affect mountain roads, trails and visibility. Avoid tight schedules and confirm access locally before departure.

PRE-DAWNSTART EARLY

The sunrise trek begins in darkness. Warm layers, sturdy footwear, a headlamp and local guidance are essential for a responsible trip.

PERMITS: Arunachal Pradesh requires an Inner Line Permit for domestic visitors and the applicable Protected Area Permit for foreign visitors. Verify current requirements and local security guidance before travelling.
BORDER-AREA RULE: Dong, Walong and Kibithoo sit in a sensitive frontier region. Follow local instructions, respect restricted areas and ask before photographing people or security installations.
