01 · THE PLACE

WHERE FIELDS,
FORESTS & CULTURE
MOVE TOGETHER.

Ziro is a green plateau in Arunachal Pradesh, known for its Apatani cultural landscape, terraced paddy fields and distinctive rice-fish cultivation.

The experience is quieter than a pass-to-pass road trip. Here, the villages, fields and forest trails are the destination.

03 · THE ROAD

TAKE THE
SCENIC WAY.

ITANAGAR→BANDERDEWA→ZIRO

Ziro is part of Arunachal Tourism's Itanagar–Ziro–Daporijo–Aalo–Pasighat circuit. Road conditions and travel times can change, so build some flexibility into the journey.

04 · PLAN SMART

WHEN TO GO.
WHAT TO KNOW.

OCTOBER – APRILCOOLER MONTHS

A popular broad travel window in Arunachal Pradesh. Expect changing conditions and cooler nights.

MARCH – JUNEGREEN SEASON

Warmer days and lush valley landscapes. Plan outdoor activities around weather.

JULY – SEPTEMBERMONSOON

Heavy rain can affect roads and access to attractions. Keep buffer days in your plan.

PERMITS

Domestic visitors need an Inner Line Permit for Arunachal Pradesh. Foreign visitors require the applicable Protected Area Permit.

TRAVEL SLOW

Ziro rewards time. Leave room for village visits, weather changes and unplanned stops.

ROAD & WEATHER

Mountain weather can change quickly. Check current road conditions before departure.

RESPECT LOCAL LIFE

Ask before photographing people or entering private spaces, and follow local guidance in villages and cultural sites.

Official information can change. Verify permits, weather and local restrictions before travelling.
