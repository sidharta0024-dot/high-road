01 · THE PLACE

WHERE THE
HILLS HAVE A PULSE.

Shillong is the capital of Meghalaya and a natural starting point for exploring the Khasi Hills. The city mixes mountain roads, cafés, live music, viewpoints and a strong local culture.

From Umiam Lake and Laitlum to waterfalls and the road south toward Sohra, Shillong works best as a base for a wider Meghalaya road trip.

04 · PLAN SMART

WHEN TO GO.
WHAT TO KNOW.

OCTOBER – APRILCLEARER DAYS

A strong window for road trips, viewpoints and longer days outside, although mountain weather can still change quickly.

MAY – SEPTEMBERMONSOON

Expect lush landscapes and dramatic rain. Build flexibility into the itinerary for wet roads and changing conditions.

ALL YEARLOCAL RHYTHM

Shillong is more than a sightseeing stop. Leave room for food, music, markets and unplanned city wandering.

SAFETY: Meghalaya authorities issued a tourist advisory in August 2026 asking visitors to remain vigilant and follow official instructions. Check current advisories before travel.
ROAD RULE: Start early, allow buffers and check local weather and road conditions before heading toward Sohra, Dawki or more remote viewpoints.
