01 · THE PLACE

WHERE THE VALLEY
GETS QUIET.

Mechuka sits in a high mountain valley in Arunachal Pradesh, shaped by the Siyom River, broad meadows, steep hills and the communities who call the valley home.

The journey is long and the destination is deliberately unhurried. Mechuka is for travellers who want mountain scenery, village life and a road trip with room to breathe.

03 · THE ROAD

THE ROAD
IS PART OF IT.

AALO→MECHUKA

Arunachal Tourism promotes the Aalo–Mechuka circuit. The road journey is long and conditions can change with weather, landslides and other local factors, so HIGH ROAD trips should be planned with buffer time.

04 · PLAN SMART

WHEN TO GO.
WHAT TO KNOW.

MAY – AUGUSTGREEN VALLEY

Official Mechukha tourism material highlights May–August for a greener travel season. Weather can still affect road access.

OCTOBER – DECEMBERDRIER & COOLER

Autumn and early winter can bring clearer days and colder nights. Pack for temperature changes.

JANUARY – FEBRUARYSNOW POSSIBILITY

Winter can bring snowfall and colder conditions. Check current road and local access information before departure.

PERMITS: Arunachal Pradesh requires an Inner Line Permit for domestic visitors and the applicable Protected Area Permit for foreign visitors. Some border-area activities and treks may require additional permissions. Verify the current requirements before travelling.
ROAD SMART: Official tourism guidance advises avoiding night travel on the long mountain journey, carrying warm clothing and allowing extra time for weather or road delays.
