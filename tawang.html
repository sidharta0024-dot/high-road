THE PLACE

WHERE THE ROAD
MEETS THE HIMALAYAS.

Tawang sits in western Arunachal Pradesh at about 3,500 metres, surrounded by high mountain landscapes and the traditions of the Monpa community. The journey is part of the experience, with dramatic passes, valleys, lakes and monasteries along the way.

For HIGH ROAD, Tawang is a road-trip destination first: slow down, take the views seriously, and leave room for the unexpected.

01HIGH ALTITUDE

Tawang town is around 3,500 m above sea level.

02MONPA CULTURE

A living cultural landscape shaped by Monpa traditions and Tibetan Buddhist heritage.

03BIG ROAD TRIP

Sela Pass, mountain valleys and high-altitude sites make the journey a major part of the trip.

PLAN SMART

BEFORE YOU GO

BEST SEASONS

March–June is generally pleasant. September–November is known for crisp air and visibility. Winter brings heavy snowfall and possible pass closures.

PERMITS

Indian travellers need an Inner Line Permit for Arunachal Pradesh. Some high-altitude/border locations have additional clearance requirements.

ACCLIMATISE

Tawang is high altitude. Plan a sensible route, allow time to acclimatise and follow current local travel and weather advisories.

ROAD CONDITIONS

Mountain weather can change quickly. Check current road conditions and local guidance before heading to high passes.

Travel details should be checked against current official guidance before departure.
