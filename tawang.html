01 · THE PLACE

WHERE THE ROAD MEETS THE HIMALAYAS.

Tawang sits in western Arunachal Pradesh at around 3,500 metres. The landscape mixes high passes, alpine lakes, mountain roads and the living traditions of the Monpa community.

It is the kind of destination where the journey is not filler between attractions. The road is the attraction.

03 · THE ROAD

THE JOURNEY
IS PART OF IT.

GUWAHATI→BHALUKPONG→BOMDILA→DIRANG→SELA→TAWANG

For a road journey, the official district guidance recommends an overnight halt at Bomdila or Dirang for altitude acclimatisation before crossing Sela Pass. Current weather and road conditions can affect the route.

04 · PLAN SMART

WHEN TO GO.
WHAT TO KNOW.

MARCH – JUNESPRING / SUMMER

Generally pleasant conditions and clearer travel windows. Rhododendrons may be in bloom.

SEPTEMBER – NOVEMBERAUTUMN

Crisp air, clearer mountain views and a popular post-monsoon travel window.

DECEMBER – FEBRUARYWINTER

Heavy snowfall and freezing conditions at high passes. Some routes may close temporarily.

PERMITS

Indian nationals need an Inner Line Permit for Arunachal Pradesh. Foreign nationals require a Protected Area Permit. Bumla and Madhuri Lake require additional clearance.

ACCLIMATISE

Tawang is high altitude. The district guidance recommends acclimatisation before higher passes and border areas. Follow current local advice.

ROAD & WEATHER

Mountain weather can change quickly. Check current road conditions and official advisories before travelling to high passes.

RESPECT THE LAND

Do not walk on frozen lakes. Follow local instructions and keep the region clean. Single-use plastics are banned across Tawang district.

Official information can change. Verify permits, weather, road status and local restrictions before departure.
