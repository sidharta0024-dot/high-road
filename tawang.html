Tawang sits in western Arunachal Pradesh at around 3,500 metres. It is a landscape of high passes, alpine lakes, monasteries and Monpa cultural traditions, with the road itself becoming part of the story.

THE JOURNEY
IS PART OF IT.

GUWAHATI→BHALUKPONG→BOMDILA→DIRANG→SELA→TAWANG

The official district guide lists the Guwahati / Tezpur route via Bhalukpong, Bomdila, Dirang and Sela Pass. Current road and weather conditions should always be checked before departure.

04 · PLAN SMART

BEFORE YOU
HIT THE ROAD.

01

WHEN TO GO

March–June: generally pleasant conditions. September–November: clearer autumn conditions. Winter: heavy snowfall and possible pass closures.

02

PERMITS

Indian visitors need an Inner Line Permit for Arunachal Pradesh. Foreign visitors require a Protected Area Permit. Some high-altitude/border sites need additional clearance.

03

ACCLIMATISE

Tawang is high altitude. Take the climb seriously, allow time to acclimatise and follow current local safety guidance.

04

CHECK THE ROAD

Mountain weather can change quickly. Check current road conditions and local advisories before heading to high passes.

TRAVEL NOTE · Always verify permits, road conditions and local advisories before departure.
